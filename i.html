King's Day Celebration



Celebrate the Dutch royal family on King’s Day, the country’s most lively national holiday. Held annually on April 27, the Netherlands turns orange in honor of King Willem-Alexander. Street parties, music festivals, and citywide flea markets create a festive atmosphere in cities across the country, making it an unforgettable experience for locals and tourists alike.

Opening Period: April 27 annually

Location: Across the Netherlands
